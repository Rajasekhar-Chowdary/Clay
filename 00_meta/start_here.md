# Start Here — The Confidence On-Ramp

> Read this when you sit down and don't know where to begin, or when you feel "not ready."

## The truth about confidence

Confidence is the **residue of having done the work** — not a thing you get beforehand.
You will not feel ready from reading. You'll feel ready after you've watched Clay enrich a
row, hit a wall, and fixed it. Three times. Reps, not affirmation.

**Beat the all-or-nothing trap:** no 3-hour marathons. One 25–45 min sitting, one concept,
a win every time. Missing a day ruins nothing. The only failure is not doing the next rep.

---

## The Confidence Ladder

Do these in order. Don't look at the next rung until the current one is done.

- [ ] **Rep 0 (2 min):** `cd 07_portfolio/03_crm_audit && python3 demo.py` — watch your own
      code produce real output. Win = "I made a working thing."
- [ ] **Rep 1 (20 min):** Clay — 5 rows, Apollo column only. Watch enrichment work once.
      Steps: `01_beginner/02_table_columns_credits.md`. Win = emails appeared.
- [ ] **Rep 2 (45 min):** 25 rows, Apollo → Hunter waterfall, calculate coverage %.
      Steps: `01_beginner/05_quiz_phase1.md`. Win = a real number in `progress_tracker.md`.
- [ ] **Rep 3 (45 min):** Add one Claygent column, test on 5 rows.
      Steps: `02_intermediate/03_claygent_basics.md`. Win = AI found what a database couldn't.
- [ ] **Rep 4 (45 min):** Run `analyze_coverage.py` on your Clay export. Win = provider
      breakdown table — the artifact recruiters actually want.

After Rep 4 you are no longer a beginner. You've built a real waterfall and measured it.

---

## The Loop (repeat for every rep)

```
1. Pick the smallest next rep — ONE column, ONE concept.
2. Do it in one sitting. No multi-day plans.
3. Capture proof — screenshot or the coverage number.
4. Explain it back in one sentence. Can't? That's the gap. Fix it, then continue.
5. Bring the proof back to your mentor. Review → troubleshoot → pick the next rep.
```

## After each rep, log one line

In `progress_tracker.md`:
```
Date | Rep # | What I did | Coverage % (if any) | One thing I learned
```

That log *is* your confidence, written down. Read it on the days you feel like you know nothing.
